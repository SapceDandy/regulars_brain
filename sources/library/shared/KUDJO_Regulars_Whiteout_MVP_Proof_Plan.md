**KUDJO and Regulars: MVP proof of concept for the whiteout hypotheses**

Prepared October 13, 2026 · Working gates, not achieved results

**The decision**

Add a proof gate before expanding toward the existing Mandala growth goals. Keep the eight pillars and their action IDs. For this phase, activate only the boxes needed to demonstrate one complete operating loop for each venture.

The existing 90-day goals are ten active, fee-generating KUDJO merchants and three paying Regulars venues with functioning lockers. Those measure commercial reach. The whiteout proof gate measures whether the product can carry out its defining operation with real participants, reliable delivery, favorable economics, and less continuing coordination.

The proposed center statements for this proof phase are:

- **KUDJO:** Two complementary local businesses run and repeat a paid customer journey through one coordinated workflow.
- **Regulars:** One paying venue repeatedly delivers a member occasion through one coordinated workflow, including its functioning locker program.

Use roughly four to six weeks of live operation as the initial review window. Partner acquisition, installation, unresolved software defects, or insufficient customer activity can extend the calendar. A date passing never turns an untested requirement into a pass.

**What proof of concept means here**

Technical proof shows that the loop can execute and recover correctly. Operational proof shows that real staff, merchants, customers, and creators can use it. Early commercial proof shows real payments, use, and paid continuation. Comparative effort evidence shows that the system takes over meaningful continuing coordination.

This proof does not establish that the category is new, that a small pilot has proven individual-level causal targeting, or that the full network will scale. It establishes the narrow operation that justifies testing those larger claims.

Anthropic's engineering guidance recommends beginning with a simple solution and adding agent complexity when it demonstrably improves performance. That supports one bounded operating workflow with clear facts, permitted actions, and feedback here. It does not require a multi-agent architecture. [E1]

**1. The KUDJO MVP**

Start with one city, one primary beauty-service customer segment, two complementary participating businesses, and three publishing local creators. Complementarity must be useful to the customer; selecting a second merchant just to demonstrate a transfer is insufficient.

The two businesses approve service details, real capacity, campaign limits, eligible contact channels, compensation, completion evidence, refund handling, and their participation in the partner journey. Use agreed fees; this plan does not settle the disputed fee configuration.

The minimum live loop is:

1. The merchant supplies a specific acquisition objective, usable appointment capacity, and permitted campaign terms.
2. KUDJO prepares a suitable campaign and creator brief using that actual information. The owner approves the bounded plan.
3. Customers follow tagged creator links, understand the offer, and accept appointments through the existing booking path.
4. Payment and completed-service evidence establish an eligible purchase. Fees and creator payable follow the approved terms.
5. A relevant customer who has permitted the communication can receive the complementary business's agreed next offer, using current availability. No appointment or purchase is committed without customer acceptance.
6. KUDJO records the second qualifying service, handles adjustments, reconciles amounts, and produces the owners' result note.
7. The next cycle uses the recorded outcome and updated capacity instead of requiring a new arrangement to be rebuilt manually.

Start from the existing booking, campaign, source, and purchase records. A manual receipt/completion verification or approved payout step can be an explicit pilot component, but its time counts and its automation status stays honest. Do not claim live Fidel, Square, or automatic transfer functionality solely because related code exists.

**The required adaptive behavior**

The workflow must respond to at least one material state change. For example, when the destination merchant's available slot is removed, KUDJO checks the current state, stops the unavailable offer, proposes only an allowed alternative, and preserves existing valid commitments. Another useful case is that a budget cap prevents a new incentive.

A copied partner link without availability or eligibility checks does not satisfy this part of the proof.

Use staging exercises for controlled failures and honest live records for actual transactions. A synthetic completion cannot satisfy a live commercial gate.

**2. The Regulars MVP**

Start with one paying venue, its functioning twelve-locker launch program, ten paying founding members as the initial enrollment target, and a named owner/manager and staff lead. Membership counts, shared positions, physical lockers, and seat capacity remain separate records.

The installation must have a confirmed funding arrangement, actual usable storage, and venue-approved procedures before locker benefits are sold. Preparing an event workflow while installation is pending is useful component work; it does not establish full locker-program readiness.

Use one small recurring member occasion, delivered three times over approximately six weeks. This can build on a venue's existing occasion. It does not require the large football production rollout.

The minimum live loop is:

1. Member status, locker allocation, permitted benefits, event facts, actual seat capacity, and guest rules are recorded.
2. The owner approves the occasion and its budget. Regulars produces the coordinated page/invitation, reminder sequence, and staff briefing from one set of event facts.
3. Guests request or confirm arrangements. Actual capacity governs accepted bookings, group changes, cancellations, and waitlists.
4. Staff use the operating view to deliver confirmed arrangements and log arrival, benefit use, and exceptions.
5. The agreed event or display cue occurs through authorized existing equipment or an acknowledged staff instruction. The result is recorded; a generated instruction alone is not proof of delivery.
6. Regulars produces a matched attendance, money, and effort report and coordinates the next occasion.

The future hands-free recognition goal is preserved. For this pilot, staff may verify and record arrival without requiring guests to perform an arrival tap. That manual observation is counted as staff effort. Automated passive identity or presence detection is a separate unproven capability.

**The required adaptive behavior**

When a group cancels, for example, Regulars releases the actual seats according to the approved rules, offers them to an eligible waitlisted party if one exists, records any accepted replacement, and updates the staff briefing and affected guest communications.

An event reminder plus a static slide does not satisfy this part of the proof. At least one meaningful operating change must propagate correctly through the connected workflow.

**4. Proposed proof gates**

The numerical thresholds below are management targets selected for this experiment, not published industry benchmarks. All current results are “Not run / not established.” Adjust the recruitment and volume targets after the first two weeks of actual participation, documenting the reason before the next test. Do not lower a requirement simply to declare the completed test a success.

| Gate | KUDJO target | Regulars target | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Participants | Two approved complementary businesses and three publishing creators | One paying venue with functioning lockers and ten paying members | Agreements, approved inventory, published work, and actual payment records |
| Real use | At least 20 paid, completed purchases across the pilot, including at least five eligible destination-business completions from the partner journey | Three delivered occasions; at least five of the ten initial members attend two or more occasions | Unique matched purchase/service records; reconciled attendance and entitlement records |
| Money integrity | All sampled purchase, fee, payable, refund, and settlement records reconcile under the agreed rules | Member receipts, earned venue/platform revenue, refunds, inventory obligations, and Regulars fees reconcile | Ledger and receipt references; unexplained differences prevent a pass |
| Reliability | All mandatory payment, authorization, availability, and adjustment acceptance cases pass; the material state change is handled correctly | Confirmed seat/locker commitments are honored; required exception cases and the material state change are handled correctly | Case evidence and actual fulfillment, not screenshots of an apparently successful interface |
| Coordination automation | At least 80% of the prespecified routine digital coordination cases in the last cycle finish without founder correction | The same 80% threshold, with staff continuing to perform physical hospitality | A frozen case definition, attempted cases, outcomes, founder corrections, and exception log |
| Continuing effort | At least 50% less total coordination time per comparable completed journey than the manual baseline | At least 50% less total coordination time per comparable occasion, also reporting attendee volume | Founder + owner + staff coordination minutes, with setup/build time shown separately |
| Economics | Positive fully costed observed campaign contribution for the merchant and KUDJO; unresolved displacement remains explicit | Positive fully costed observed program contribution for venue and platform; seat displacement and fulfillment obligations included | Costs, labor valuation, collected/earned fees, and a comparison or sensitivity analysis |
| Repeat payment | Both merchants pay the earned pilot charges and continue into a second paid cycle | Venue pays its first operating fee and pays for the next operating period; member renewal is tracked when actually due | Cleared receipts and continued use, rather than verbal enthusiasm |

Failure on a critical money, access, inventory, or delivery control holds the relevant live workflow until corrected. Customer demand, operating leverage, and economics are assessed separately: technical success does not compensate for absent willingness to pay.

A small pilot's attributed sales are not automatically additional sales. Positive observed contribution is early feasibility evidence. A stronger incremental-profit claim requires a credible comparison and enough observations; matched periods with changing conditions should remain qualified. The paid continuation decision is useful commercial evidence, but it is not a randomized estimate of lift.

**5. Measure the automation fairly**

Define routine coordination cases before each test. A case is a complete useful outcome, not an individual message or model call. Examples include a confirmed appointment carried through its communication sequence, an eligible partner handoff, or a group change propagated through inventory, guest messages, and the staff briefing.

Record every attempted eligible case. Do not pad the denominator with trivial notifications, discard failures, or exclude normal cases merely because a human had to repair them. Owner approval of the initial bounded plan is allowed; repeated founder editing or dispatch counts as intervention.

Use at least 20 prespecified routine cases across the measured final operating cycle for the 80% gate. If the sample is smaller, report the count and keep the gate incomplete. Staff service, merchant service completion, and explicitly manual settlement are separate human responsibilities; they are not represented as automated digital work.

For the time comparison, record founder, owner, and staff coordination minutes. Moving work from Devon to the venue does not count as removing it. Compare similar volume and complexity, show both total time and a useful per-outcome measure, and retain setup/build effort separately.

Value delivery labor at an explicit agreed planning rate when calculating full cost. Unknown costs stay unknown. Venue dues are not Regulars revenue; merchant sales are not KUDJO revenue; annual upfront receipts are not all earned monthly profit.

**6. The build and operating sequence**

| Phase | KUDJO | Regulars | End-of-phase decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prepare the test | Confirm pair, terms, capacity and evidence; resolve the selected pilot's purchase/settlement blockers | Confirm paying venue, funded usable lockers, offer, staff lead and one occasion | Can the actual promised service be delivered and measured? |
| Run the manual baseline | Operate the first agreed journey and record every coordination task and cost | Operate the first occasion and record guest, staff, founder and money evidence | Which repeated coordination is expensive enough to automate? |
| Automate the narrow loop | Reuse the existing records to run the approved campaign, partner handoff, state-change response and result note | Reuse the event/member facts to run bookings, communications, staff briefing, state-change response and result note | Can the workflow operate correctly with less founder correction? |
| Repeat and compare | Run the next paid campaign cycle; reconcile the mature purchases and adjustments | Deliver occasions two and three across ordinary staff shifts; collect the next venue payment when due | Do the defined delivery, effort, demand and economic gates pass? |

Work on both ventures in parallel using the existing daily blocks. The live review window starts when each actual pilot is ready. With no live partner, work can establish component readiness, but participant and commercial gates remain incomplete.

Use three recurring habits across both products: check operating failures each day, reconcile records and costs weekly, and hold a short weekly evidence review. The week's build work addresses the largest failure that prevents the next gate from passing.

**7. What to build first**

KUDJO's first build work is the trustworthy paid/completed purchase, capacity, fee, and adjustment path for the selected pilot. Add one bounded partner handoff and a campaign operating view that uses those records. Test one material state change before opening the live journey.

Regulars' first build work is the member/locker record, capacity-governed arrangements, and staff operating view. Generate the occasion's guest communication and staff briefing from one approved event record. Test a group cancellation or comparable meaningful change before delivering the recurring occasion.

These are the minimum operation modules. A new POS replacement, broad merchant network, custom presence hardware, betting system, full television-control product, AI influencer studio, and third-party data marketplace are not required to establish this particular proof. Keep the larger roadmap; activate its next item when it removes a demonstrated constraint.

**8. The evidence packet at the review**

The final packet contains the approved scope and terms; participant/payment records; a timeline of the complete operating loop; mandatory case results; delivered-result references; the adaptive case; all routine attempted cases and founder interventions; time and cost comparisons; reconciliation; and paid continuation evidence.

A labeled demo can demonstrate the interface. It cannot substitute for the packet's real operation and commercial evidence.

The review ends with one of three decisions: pass the narrow proof and test replication; hold the specific missing gate; or change the weak workflow/offer and run a new documented test. Growth toward ten KUDJO merchants and three Regulars venues follows the operation that survives this review.
