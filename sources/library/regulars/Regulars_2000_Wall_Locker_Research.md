# Regulars: $2,000 total installed wall-locker research

Research date: October 12, 2026. Scope: twelve physical lockers, approximately ten upright bottles per locker, elevated staff access, premium neighborhood-bar appearance, founder assembly/installation, and a $2,000 customer price including founder compensation. The separate Regulars platform fee remains $500/month.

## Decision

The IKEA frames and locking inserts are affordable, but the stock wall-mounted configuration does not meet the proposed bottle load. A reinforced version remains a design-development option, not an installation we can currently promise as safe or code-compliant. Retain the IKEA locking inserts if bottle fit works; investigate either a genuinely independent structural retrofit or custom plywood cabinet bodies.

Do not purchase the full twelve-locker setup yet. Purchase one four-cube frame and one locking insert, nominally $74.99 before tax, for dimensional, assembly, access and finish checks. This does not authorize an overload test or public installation. These parts can become part of the final system if the design uses them.

The $2,000 total price may work at a favorable site with founder labor, but published product prices do not establish an installed quote. The illustrative full-IKEA retrofit budget leaves approximately $380 for founder labor, not $900 or $2,000. A higher review or reinforcement bill can eliminate most of that pay.

## How the source requirement is handled

Four ten-reference evidence groups appear at the end: product/load/fit; reinforcement; budget/compensation; installation/legal conditions. Each reference states what it supports. There are 30 distinct reference entries across those groups; some sources are reused because the same product specifications affect several decisions.

These are ten-document evidence groups, not ten independent confirmations of every sentence. Only IKEA establishes the exact IKEA product specification. Arithmetic is calculated from stated inputs. No retrieved source certifies our custom retrofit or promises a $2,000 installed result. The literal request for ten reputable sources independently proving every conclusion is therefore not met; padding the bibliography with unrelated pages would not improve the evidence.

Manufacturer product sheets/manuals, government guidance and engineering organizations are prioritized. Supplier load ratings apply to their stated products and conditions, not automatically to the complete cabinet/wall assembly. Published contractor pricing is an adjacent comparison, not a commercial locker quote.

### Factory load limits

The current frame page lists 29 lb maximum load per shelf and 44 lb 1 oz maximum wall-cabinet load. The assembly illustration separately marks 13 kg at each upper compartment and 20 kg total contents for a wall-hung four-cube unit [01, 03]. Follow both the local shelf limit and whole-unit wall limit; do not add the shelf limits together to create a higher hanging rating.

IKEA also says wall-hung capacity depends on wall material. That condition does not mean a stronger wall authorizes exceeding the furniture rating. No published higher rating for this retrofit was located.

At even 2.5 lb per full bottle, forty bottles in a four-locker module weigh 100 lb. That exceeds the approximately 44 lb wall-cabinet limit before considering insert weight. At 3 lb, the bottles weigh 120 lb; at 4 lb, 160 lb. Therefore the stock hanging configuration is unsuitable for the requested capacity.

### Proposed design brief

For bottle types verified at no more than 4 lb each, request a design for at least 50 lb working CONTENTS per compartment and 600 lb simultaneous contents across twelve, plus actual cabinet/support weight. This is a proposed service-load requirement, not a certified rating, building-code minimum, proof-test load or selected safety factor. The designer must choose appropriate design methods and factors.

The reviewer must consider:

- All compartments full.
- Left half full and right half empty, then the reverse.
- Upper compartments full and lower compartments empty.
- One upper end compartment full.
- Contents clustered toward the front of a compartment.
- Doors operating, foreseeable pulls/bumps and long-term shelf deflection.
- Cabinet twisting, connection withdrawal, vertical loading and movement restraints.

At the 4 lb assumption, six full compartments contribute 240 lb concentrated on one side or in the upper row. Do not depend on contents in the opposite half to balance it.

The center of the contents sits away from the wall. That creates outward leverage as well as downward force. A wall-anchor rating cannot be treated as a pure vertical-weight allowance for the complete installation. Connection behavior depends on the member, fastener, embedment and loading direction [11, 13, 15].

## 3. Will ten bottles fit?

It is not established. IKEA provides outside insert dimensions, not the exact usable footprint with the door, hinges and lock installed [02, 04].

Packaging suppliers show materially different 750 ml bottle profiles. Berlin's searchable product specifications report Juniper at 3.5 in diameter with 650 g empty glass, Aspect at 3.299 in with 680 g glass, and Luca at 3.74 in diameter/9.68 in high with 650 g glass [08–10]. These examples are not the venue's actual bottles and are not purchase recommendations; live pages did not expose all specifications in the extracted body, so physical measurement takes precedence.

For illustration, a simple 4-by-3 rectangular layout of 3.5 in diameter bottles requires 14 in width and 10.5 in depth BEFORE retrieval clearance. It will not fit a 13 in-wide insert. A 3-by-3 arrangement contains nine, not ten. Staggering can change packing, so this does not prove a universal nine-bottle maximum.

### Prototype acceptance

1. Select ten full bottles representing the largest/widest/tallest permitted menu options.
2. Measure the assembled usable width, height and depth, including lock and hinge intrusion.
3. Verify doors close without bottle contact.
4. Retrieve rear bottles with normal staff handling and record what must be moved.
5. Confirm liners and position labels still fit.
6. Weigh bottles and the sample components.
7. Record the permitted bottle envelope and maximum contents weight.

Use a supported bench setup for fit checks. Do not conduct a hanging overload trial. If ten bottles do not fit, use larger custom compartments/doors or reduce the stated physical capacity. A stronger mounting system cannot fix insufficient interior space.

Commercially, retain twelve physical lockers and up to four rentable member positions per locker unless pricing is deliberately revised. A ten-bottle structural capacity is not permission to sell ten shared memberships. Define bottle allowances and identification rules for each position before promising that capacity to members.

## 4. How IKEA assembly works

Use the complete manuals for the delivered article numbers [03, 04]. They are illustrated instructions, not generic cabinet-building plans.

### Frame

The frame uses factory panels, dowels, dividers and supplied screws. The illustrated sequence shows fitting the shelves/dividers and closing the outer frame, then installing the supplied wall fittings. A hammer/mallet is used only where the manual indicates seating dowels; screws are tightened with the appropriate screwdriver. The manual illustrates two-person handling.

Do not hammer the whole cabinet together, overdrive screws in particleboard, invent extra structural holes or assume added glue raises its rating. Assemble on a protected surface and check for damage, squareness and joint closure.

### Locking insert

The manual shows a foldable side/back enclosure, alignment within the cube, fastening it to the frame, assembling the door hardware and lock/strike, and fitting the door. It supplies enclosure/door parts, not a heavy-duty replacement shelf. The bottles sit on the host cabinet's shelf.

Preserve the factory door geometry and lock operation. Finish the sample before assuming primer, paint or veneer will survive bar use. For surface preparation, use the finish manufacturer's instructions, ventilation and cure requirements [25, 29]; the nominal finish allowance below is not a finished-shop quote.

## 5. Reinforcement options

| Approach | What it changes | Remaining issue | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Better wall anchors only | Wall connection | Factory shelves, joints and wall-cabinet limit remain | Insufficient evidence |
| Rated brackets/platform beneath each frame | Supports the assembly at its underside | Upper shelves and carcass load transfer remain unverified | Mounting component only |
| Plywood sheet added behind the stock unit | May improve stiffness if properly connected | Connections into thin/hollow components may govern | Not a certified upgrade |
| Independent structural retrofit | New shelves/support members transfer bottle loads into their own back/frame and wall connections | Needs design, fit and fabrication validation | Feasible concept to quote |
| Custom plywood bodies with IKEA inserts | Purpose-built shelf/joint/back geometry; IKEA doors retained if compatible | Inserts are officially dimensioned for KALLAX; custom fit and final design must be checked | Preferred development route |

### How a meaningful retrofit functions

Bottle → new load-bearing shelf/support → structural cabinet back or frame → specified bracket/rail → specified connections → verified building structure.

The IKEA parts can remain as enclosure/appearance components where the design permits. Every relevant shelf load must enter the NEW support system; a stronger platform only at the bottom does not establish that the upper shelf is stronger.

Possible design components include supported plywood shelf inserts, structural dividers or steel members, a properly connected structural back, concealed brackets and restraints against outward rotation. Their sizes, quantities, joints and fastening schedules are to be specified. No adhesive, angle-bracket count or screw length is prescribed here as an approved solution.

### Actual support products to investigate

- Rockler Heavy-Duty Steel Shelf Brackets, black: current detailed specification says 1,000 lb PER PAIR, despite an inconsistent headline suggesting per bracket. Use the detailed specification. The 12 × 18 in pair is regularly $34.99; the retrieved page showed $27.99 promotional pricing. We budget the regular price [16]. The rating does not establish the wall, shelf or complete assembly capacity.
- Federal Brace Fortis: concealed floating support intended for stud-connected applications; manufacturer spec states 500 lb carrying capacity per bracket under its stated design. Retrieved variants start at $209.99 each [17, 18]. The instructions require fastening to studs and building the cabinet frame around the supports. This illustrates a much more expensive concealed-support route; it is not the default budgeted bracket.
- Cabinet-grade veneer-core plywood: Columbia describes actual wood veneer core layers and decorative faces [14]. Select the exact core, thickness and design properties; a premium face veneer or PureBond name alone is not a structural load rating [12].

Three Rockler bracket pairs in the budget are a COST SCENARIO, not an approved quantity or layout. Brackets may need to be enclosed in a finished soffit/surround to obtain the intended appearance. They cannot simply disappear behind a flat cabinet if their geometry conflicts with the wall or interior.

## 7. Conditions for installing in a Central Texas bar

No retrieved rule makes a cabinet legal solely because it weighs a certain amount or has a particular advertised bracket rating. The actual site and scope matter.

| Issue | Required resolution before installation |
|---|---|
| Building/landlord authorization | Written permission for placement and wall attachments; agree on restoration responsibility |
| Wall condition | Determine wood/steel studs, masonry, condition, utilities and whether backing/opening the wall is necessary |
| Permits | Ask the actual city to classify this cabinet/attachment scope; do not assume an Austin exemption applies elsewhere |
| Austin | Cabinets/similar commercial finish work may be exempt, but applicable codes still apply [19] |
| Round Rock/Hutto | Use their commercial permit/building departments for the actual site determination [21, 22] |
| Your general installation work | Texas does not require a GC state license; Austin registration applies when assigned to permitted work [20] |
| Lock installation | Resolve DPS pre-keyed-lockset exemption conditions; no rekeying/internal manipulation; otherwise use appropriately licensed service [23] |
| Accessibility | Determine whether the installation is employee work-area storage or customer locker storage; staff-controlled keys alone do not settle the classification [06, 07] |
| Applicable accessible lockers | TAS 225.2.1 requires 5%, minimum one of each type; TAS 811 addresses floor space, reach and operable parts [06, 07] |
| Small project | Less than $50,000 generally avoids TDLR registration/review, not covered TAS compliance [05] |
| Circulation | Check protruding-object, accessible-route and exit effects; elevated cabinets can project into circulation paths [06] |
| Electrical | Resolve installation/cord routing and any licensed electrical work; do not budget new wiring as free |
| Alcohol | Venue keeps lawful sourcing, identification stamps, control and service under its permit [30]; confirm program structure with TABC |
| Fire/storage | Have the venue resolve actual bottle quantity, strengths and location with the fire authority; no universal approval is claimed here |
| Tax | Confirm tangible equipment versus real-property improvement classification, sales permit, invoice tax and purchase/resale treatment [24] |

Chest-level access is a layout objective, not automatic accessibility compliance. Check staff reach using actual bottle heights; do not put the bottom of a two-row cabinet at chest height and assume the upper row is usable.

The attachment can be concealed, but it cannot be promised damage-free. Structural mounting normally requires penetrations. Adhesive wall mounting, suction devices or ordinary furniture straps are not established suspension methods for this load.

The review recommendation is our risk/control decision for a heavily loaded public-facing custom installation, not a claim that every cabinet installation legally requires a PE stamp. The building official determines documentation requirements for the actual scope.

## 8. Execution and finish acceptance

1. Assemble one factory sample, measure/weigh it, and test the ten-bottle fit on a safely supported bench.
2. Make one finished door/surround sample: walnut exterior, matte black door treatment if suitable, number plate and functioning factory lock.
3. Survey the selected venue's wall, access, circulation and lighting connection.
4. Request a reviewed retrofit design AND a custom-body alternative; ask for material quantities, attachment schedule, working capacities and uneven-load checks.
5. Replace allowances with actual supplier/installer/design prices. Recalculate the $2,000 residual before offering a fixed price.
6. Assemble/fabricate only to the reviewed design and factory manuals. Use a helper and suitable temporary lifting support.
7. Complete the reviewer-specified acceptance/inspection procedure; do not invent a proof-test load.
8. Inspect finish under the venue's lighting: consistent door gaps, square trim, clean edges, concealed support/wiring, no binding locks, easy retrieval and sealed spill surfaces.
9. Record the contents-weight limit, bottle-size envelope, staff-only key policy and inspection/maintenance instructions.
10. Document the delivered installation and activate the digital membership/bottle records.

Expansion should add another reviewed module beside the original. Stacking above it changes total weight, wall loading and staff reach and requires a new check.
