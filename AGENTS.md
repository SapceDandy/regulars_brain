# Instructions for Regulars collaborators

Read README.md, knowledge/current-concept.md and knowledge/decisions.md before answering strategy questions or proposing changes. Then consult the relevant specification and indexed originals.

Authority order: the owner's latest explicit decisions; current maintained concept and dated decisions; relevant source documents with their dates; older proposals and scenario models. If a newer owner instruction contradicts the synthesis, update the decision log and affected summaries. Do not quietly turn a recommendation into a decision.

Regulars adds a connected digital experience to a physical venue. Events and lockers introduce the system; do not reduce the business to storage rental or a generic events agency. The current MVP is one venue, twelve functioning lockers, member/guest and staff workflows, seating requests, basic AI coordination and two featured campaigns monthly. The venue service target is $500/month; installation target is about $2,500, subject to a real site-specific quote. Member prices are tests, not adopted prices.

Keep planned features, observed implementation and measured outcomes separate. Do not claim an app works, a venue signed, attendance improved, a structural design is safe, or a promotion is lawful without current evidence. This is a knowledge repository, not an application repository.

Preserve sources byte-for-byte. Add dated decisions and evidence with source paths. Cite originals when answering detailed questions; read the original spreadsheet or visual for calculation/layout questions rather than relying on text extraction. Shared files contain multiple ventures; use only the relevant Regulars sections.

Mark fresh recommendations as proposed. Keep prices and unit economics explicit about assumptions, payer, recipient and direct costs. Avoid guaranteed seats unless allocated inventory and venue-approved terms support that promise. Keep alcohol handling with authorized venue staff and unapproved points/wagering or game-triggered alcohol concepts out of pilot implementation.

Do not put live patron records, payment secrets, identity documents or private credentials in this repository. Use synthetic examples in specs. Maintain INDEX.md and catalog/source-manifest.json when adding sources. Run scripts/validate_knowledge.py after documentation changes. A repo is available as context when opened or queried; do not promise automatic universal memory or silent background synchronization.
