 Block rules
 Start each reserved block with its named output. End by saving evidence and the next action. Keep the
 campaign/member board visible. Do not compensate for a missed block by removing sleep. Use the named evening
 catch-up block; defer the lowest-value optional feature if demand or operations need time.


 Launch dependencies
 Regulars: resolve the exact venue supply, custody, service, membership and tax model before program payments.
 TABC mixed-beverage guidance has supplier/invoice restrictions and forbids outside customer alcohol; it does not
 itself approve this locker structure. Review the written flow with the venue and TABC or qualified counsel.

 KUDJO: transactions support agreed attribution, not proof of a social-video view or causation. Fidel requires linked
 cards at program locations and an accepted use case. Use merchant booking/receipt evidence while approvals,
 coverage and costs are pending. Owned virtual presenters use real approved footage and clear commercial
 disclosure; no invented personal visits, results or testimonials. FTC guidance addresses virtual influencers.
