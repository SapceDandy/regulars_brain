# Long-term direction: curated social and dating experiences

Status: owner-requested strategic direction, October 15, 2026. The dating and social opportunity is part of the long-term concept. The operating details below are proposals to validate after the core membership and venue system works.

Source: Devon Dudley's October 15, 2026 instruction to add this direction to the Regulars concept repository.

## The concept

Regulars can eventually function as a premium social and dating platform built around its network of real venues. A women-facing community would let women voluntarily sign up to meet vetted male Regulars members through invitations, dates, and hosted gatherings. Eligible men would enter through a membership and vetting process. Their participation in paid memberships, lockers, and premium venue experiences would demonstrate willingness to spend on those experiences and provide an initial eligibility signal. Identity and conduct vetting would remain a separate requirement.

The membership perk is participation in a curated community and the ability to invite other consenting participants to real experiences. Each woman controls whether she is discoverable, receives invitations, and accepts a particular invitation. Membership purchases no entitlement to a response, date, attendance, or relationship.

## Why this belongs in Regulars

The venue network supplies the places to meet. Lockers and memberships supply recurring hosts, a reason to return, and infrastructure for arranging a visit. The social layer supplies another reason to choose a venue, bring guests, and maintain a membership. Together, they could turn participating bars into recurring destinations for dates and social gatherings.

For members, the potential benefit is easier access to curated introductions and organized nights out. For women who join, the proposed value is choice among vetted members and well-organized experiences at known venues. For venues, the potential benefit is additional visits, food and beverage spending, premium bookings, and repeat attendance. These are commercial hypotheses to test; no attendance, revenue, or dating outcomes are established.

## Community acquisition

The owner's proposed starting route is through existing venue communities, including women connected to hosts, promoters, and the Cowboy-venue example discussed in conversation, plus direct invitations to women interested in a premium dating community. A venue or promoter could distribute an opt-in signup invitation. Each participant would supply her own information and choose to join; staff affiliation or presence at a venue would not enroll anyone automatically.

No partnership with the example venue or its staff is implied. The existing exclusion of Rowdy Cowboy from the initial three-month venue pipeline remains in force; a later social expansion is a separate opportunity.

## Proposed experience

1. A woman chooses to join the community, sets her interests and invitation preferences, and controls participation.
2. A male Regulars member separately opts into the dating/social layer and completes its vetting process. Locker ownership or payment alone does not complete vetting.
3. Eligible participants express interest or send a bounded invitation for a date, small-group outing, hosted table, or curated mixer. The recipient can accept, decline, block, or report.
4. A mutually accepted plan becomes a venue visit, subject to venue availability and confirmed booking terms. Contact information is shared only with permission.
5. Regulars measures attendance, participant satisfaction, repeat visits, and venue economics to determine whether the experience creates value.

For bar-based experiences, the proposed participant minimum is 21+. Specific vetting standards, staffing, moderation, privacy controls, pricing, and venue responsibilities must be designed before a live trial. Ordinary venue membership remains usable without joining the dating community. No patron lists, profiles, or identity documents belong in this documentation repository.

## Timing and proof

This is a later expansion after the core venue, locker, membership, and staff workflows have demonstrated reliability. It adds no dating profiles, matching engine, recruitment campaign, or dating-related launch requirement to the current MVP.

A proposed first experiment is a small, manually coordinated event with a willing partner venue and participants who expressly opted in. Evaluate invitation acceptance, accepted-invitation-to-attendance conversion, repeat participation, member renewal interest, participant feedback, and incremental venue contribution after hosting costs. Event purchases alone do not prove incremental revenue; compare with an appropriate baseline. Set trial thresholds before recruiting, and expand only if participants and the venue both want to continue.

The longer-term hypothesis is a reinforcing cycle: better experiences attract willing participants, successful gatherings strengthen membership value, and repeat spending makes the venue network more attractive to additional partners. Network growth and profitable operations remain unproven.
