The imported Regulars Mandala has eight pillars and sixty-four actions. Their status is historical/unverified; inclusion is not proof of completion. The original multi-venture action guide also includes KUDJO and studio actions. Use catalog/mandala-actions.csv for the Regulars-only list.

## Current proposed sequence

1. Freeze the current concept, venue offer, responsibilities and pilot evidence requirements.
2. Select a qualified local venue, confirm decision-maker access and walk the physical site.
3. Prepare the sales kit: mobile-friendly offer, honest demo, signed-order workflow, tested payment/receipt and onboarding plan.
4. Confirm installation fit, actual quote and funding/payment terms before ordering materials.
5. Implement and rehearse the core member, staff, locker, seating and communication workflows.
6. Run two bounded campaigns, reconcile actual visits and service effort, collect renewal evidence and make a continuation decision.

These tasks are not marked complete by this import. Original calendars contain historical schedules; do not create events or assume dates are current simply because an ICS is archived.
