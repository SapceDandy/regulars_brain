# Venue offer and economics

Current working offer: one twelve-locker launch, $500/month venue service and approximately $2,500 separately quoted installation. Targets are not supplier quotes or signed contracts. The latest source proposes no additional percentage of membership or bottle sales for the pilot.

## Monthly scope to confirm in the venue order

| Deliverable | Latest working limit |
| --- | --- |
| Featured campaigns | Two approved occasions/month, possibly existing venue events |
| Event pages | Up to two dated pages and the agreed coordination flows |
| Social assets | Eight reusable assets from approved/supplied venue media; one batched revision |
| Business profile | Initial cleanup and four suitable updates/month |
| Discovery work | Initial audit and one focused information/page improvement/month |
| Reviews | Neutral request workflow and up to ten response drafts/month |
| Tools | Agreed member/pass, locker and seating tools plus basic AI FAQ/coordination |
| Reporting | Weekly snapshot where data supports it and one monthly review |

Venue supplies onsite staff, approved facts/media and physical service. Advertising, message usage, performers, professional shoots, major website changes and custom hardware need separate scoped treatment. Avoid an unlimited-labor promise. Detailed source: [latest design](../sources/library/regulars/Regulars_Seat_Led_Club_Research_and_Membership_Design.md).

## Member price tests

The $49 founding club, $69 named shared position and $99 whole locker are proposed tests. A $199 seat bundle is conditional on genuinely contracted seat inventory. Consumer membership and bottle revenue are separate from Regulars' venue service revenue. Do not report the venue's gross sales as platform revenue.

## Measure actual contribution

Venue incremental contribution = additional membership and purchase contribution attributable to the program, minus additional service, fulfillment, event and program costs. A before/after sales difference alone does not prove incrementality.

Regulars recurring contribution = collected venue fee minus software, messages, support, digital delivery and other recurring costs. Installation contribution = collected installation payment minus actual materials, labor, transport, tax and rework/contingency costs. Founder hours should be recorded and costed. Ten venues at $500/month is $5,000 gross monthly service revenue before costs, not demonstrated sustainable income.

Retain original spreadsheets for sensitivity analysis. Their scenarios and user-reported venue benchmarks are unverified planning inputs; they do not establish achieved profit or demand. Confirm actual dimensions, safe load, supplier costs and installer quote before accepting a fixed installation promise. The recommended thirty-day pilot and milestone payments in the sales kit are proposals requiring explicit agreement.
