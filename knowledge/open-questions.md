# Open questions

1. Which venue is the first pilot, and who can sign its order?
2. What exact paid member entitlement, tier prices and shared-position count will that venue approve?
3. What seat inventory can be committed, at which times, with what grace, cancellation and no-show rules?
4. What physical layout, safe load, lock/access design and installer quote support twelve functioning lockers?
5. Who funds actual installation costs, and what payment, refund, service-term and cancellation conditions will be agreed?
6. Which payment, pass, messaging and optional POS tools will be used, and which workflows are implemented versus still to build?
7. What staff access, identity checks, bottle-service records and exception process does the venue approve?
8. What communication permissions, retention and privacy procedures apply to guest data?
9. Which two initial occasions and which approved venue media/channels fit the monthly scope?
10. What renewal, contribution, guest-experience and delivery-effort thresholds justify continuing or expanding?
11. Which later entertainment/reward concepts require separate venue and current professional review before implementation?

Resolve these through real venue decisions, measurements and dated records. They do not prevent maintaining the approved concept in the meantime.
