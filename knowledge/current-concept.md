# Regulars: current concept

Status: owner-approved concept, updated October 15, 2026. The core explanation reconstructs the four-paragraph concept approved before the repository request; the long-term social and dating direction was added by a later owner instruction that day. This is not a verbatim transcript. Detailed implementation below is still a proposed specification.

## Thesis

Venues can build more consistent revenue by giving people reasons to belong, return and bring others. Regulars adds a connected digital experience around a physical venue: who is coming, their group, membership, preferences and the activity happening around them. The long-term goal is an AI system that attracts guests, coordinates visits and maintains those relationships over time. Its value must show up in repeat participation, venue economics and manageable operating effort.

## Supporting hypotheses

Recognition, status, reliable access and recurring occasions can strengthen loyalty. A locker makes the relationship visible and provides a reason to return. Defined seating privileges reduce uncertainty when planning with friends. Hosted groups and events create relationships; digital promotion attracts people and relevant invitations bring them back. AI could make that coordination affordable. These are testable hypotheses, not proven demand or causal findings.

## The complete concept

Guests can use a free pass or paid membership, plan visits with friends, request available seats and, at suitable tiers, share or exclusively use a bottle locker. Staff see the member's status, confirmed arrangements and authorized bottle records. Later, AI supports invitations, recurring gatherings and approved game-linked announcements, participation, rewards or entertainment. Partners and sponsors can provide additional benefits, with operational intelligence later. Lockers introduce the relationship; the wider connected experience supplies ongoing value. Food perks are not the membership foundation.

## Long-term social and dating value

As the venue network grows, Regulars could become a premium social and dating platform: women voluntarily join a curated community, and vetted male Regulars members can invite interested participants to dates and hosted gatherings at partner venues. Paid memberships, lockers, and premium experiences provide an initial signal of willingness to spend; identity and conduct vetting remain separate. Each participant controls invitations and participation. The proposed membership benefit is curated introductions and organized experiences, with venue value coming from additional visits, spending, and repeat gatherings. Recruitment could start through willing venue hosts, promoters, and direct opt-in signup. See [the long-term social and dating direction](long-term-social-and-dating.md) for the proposed model and proof requirements. This is a later expansion, outside the current MVP.

## MVP

Start at one venue with twelve functioning lockers and a digital member/guest workflow: memberships and renewal tracking, shared or whole locker allocation, a pass, staff lookup, bottle-service records, seating requests and confirmations, and basic AI questions, reminders and invitations. Provide two featured event campaigns monthly, bounded social and local-search support, and reporting. Working targets are $500 per venue per month and roughly $2,500 for installation, subject to actual design and quote. Venue staff handle physical service and exceptions. Expand game-night automation and partner programs after the core workflow is reliable.

## Interpretation boundaries

A request is not a guaranteed reservation. Locker capacity is separate from seat capacity. Proposed member pricing and return-on-investment scenarios are not validated willingness to pay or realized profit. The twelve-locker pilot supersedes older forty-eight-locker examples for initial scope; those examples remain expansion scenarios.
